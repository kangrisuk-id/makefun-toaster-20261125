Brief: one line, max 120 chars, a real toaster error message, judged head-to-head by an AI judge
(deadpan comedy editor). All 40 candidates below are original, written from the brief only.

no legal/lawyer angle and no crumb-tray-reclassification angle appear in the bracket.

## Judge criteria (in order — first difference decides)
1. Real error message a kitchen toaster would show its owner, and the funniest of its kind.
2. Freshness: surprise arrives from somewhere the obvious toaster puns never go.
3. Tightness: every word earns its place; as short as it can be.
4. One clear voice (deadpan / apologetic / quietly dramatic), held to the end.
5. Lands on a single read; no setup needing a second sentence.

Scores are 0–5 per criterion, in criterion order.
